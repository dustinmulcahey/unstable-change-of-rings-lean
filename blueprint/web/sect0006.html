For a comonad \(G\) on \(\mathcal{C}\), the adjunction \(U_G \dashv \operatorname {cofree}_G\) induces a monad \((T, \mu , \eta )\) on \(\mathcal{C}^G\) with \(T(X, \psi _X) = (GX, \Delta _X)\), \(\eta _X = \psi _X\), and \(\mu _X = G(\epsilon _X)\). The cosimplicial cobar complex \(C^{\bullet }_G(X)\) has \(C^n_G(X) = T^{n+1}(X)\) with coaugmentation \(d^0_{-1} = \eta _X\), cofaces \(d^i_n : T^{n+1}X \to T^{n+2}X\), and codegeneracies \(s^j_n : T^{n+2}X \to T^{n+1}X\).

The cobar complex \(C^{\bullet }_G(X)\) satisfies all five cosimplicial identities, and for every \(J \in \mathcal{C}\) the augmented simplicial hom-tower \(\operatorname {Hom}_{\mathcal{C}^G}(C^{\bullet }_G(X), G(J)) \to \operatorname {Hom}_{\mathcal{C}^G}(X, G(J))\) admits a left contraction given by \(f \mapsto G(f) \circ \Delta \).

Proof ▶

All five cosimplicial identities follow by induction on cosimplicial degree from the monad laws of \(T\). The left contraction identities follow from coassociativity and counitality of \(G\).
