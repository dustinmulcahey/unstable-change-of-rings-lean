2.1 Comonads, Coalgebras, and the Cokleisli Category

Definition 2.1 Cokleisli-to-Coalgebra Functor
✓
#

Lean declarations

  • ThesisLean.Section2_1.cokleisliToCoalgebra

For a comonad \(G\) on a category \(\mathcal{C}\), the functor \(i : \mathcal{C}_G \to \mathcal{C}^G\) sends \(X \mapsto (GX, \Delta _X)\) and a Kleisli morphism \(f : GX \to Y\) to \(G(f) \circ \Delta _X : GX \to GY\).

Definition 2.2 Coalgebra-to-Cokleisli Functor
✓
#

  • ThesisLean.Section2_1.coalgebraToCokleisli

For a comonad \(G\) on \(\mathcal{C}\), the functor \(j : \mathcal{C}^G \to \mathcal{C}_G\) sends a \(G\)-coalgebra \((X, \psi _X)\) to \(X\) and a coalgebra morphism \(f : X \to Y\) to \(f \circ \epsilon _X : GX \to Y\).

  • ThesisLean.Section2_1.cokleisliToCoalgebraFullyFaithful

The functor \(i : \mathcal{C}_G \to \mathcal{C}^G\) is fully faithful (exhibiting \(\mathcal{C}_G\) as the full subcategory of cofree coalgebras in \(\mathcal{C}^G\)), and \(j : \mathcal{C}^G \to \mathcal{C}_G\) provides the comparison functor in the opposite direction.

Proof ▶

Verified via the hom-set equivalence \(\mathcal{C}(GX, Y) \simeq \mathcal{C}^G((X,\psi _X), (GY,\Delta _Y))\) sending \(f \mapsto G(f) \circ \Delta _X \circ \psi _X\) with inverse \(g \mapsto \epsilon _Y \circ g\).
