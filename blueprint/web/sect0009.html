3.1 The Stable Case and Canonical Coalgebra Equalizer

Proposition 3.1 Canonical Equalizer Presentation of Coalgebras
✓
#

Lean declarations

  • ThesisLean.Section3_1.coalgebraCanonicalEqualizerIso

For any comonad \(G\) on \(\mathcal{C}\) and any \(G\)-coalgebra \(X = (X, \psi _X)\), the coaction \(\psi _X : X \to \operatorname {cofree}_G(X)\) exhibits \(X\) as the equalizer in \(\mathcal{C}^G\) of \(\operatorname {cofree}_G(\psi _X)\) and \(\Delta _X : \operatorname {cofree}_G(X) \rightrightarrows \operatorname {cofree}_G(GX)\).

Proof ▶

Any cone \(k : W \to \operatorname {cofree}_G(X)\) equalizing \(\operatorname {cofree}_G(\psi _X)\) and \(\Delta _X\) factors uniquely through \(X\) via \(\epsilon _X \circ k\).
