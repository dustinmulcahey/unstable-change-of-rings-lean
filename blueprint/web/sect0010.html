3.2 The Unstable Case

Theorem 3.3 Unstable Sandwich Comonad \(U_{\Sigma }\)
✓
#

Lean declarations

  • ThesisLean.Section3_2.unstableSigmaComonad

For any comonad \(U_{\Gamma }\) on \(\mathcal{C}\) and any adjunction \(F \dashv U : \mathcal{D} \to \mathcal{C}\) with unit \(\eta \) and counit \(\varepsilon \), the endofunctor \(U_{\Sigma } := F \circ U_{\Gamma } \circ U : \mathcal{D} \to \mathcal{D}\) is a comonad with counit \(\epsilon ^{\Sigma }_N = \varepsilon _N \circ F(\epsilon ^{\Gamma }_{UN})\) and comultiplication \(\Delta ^{\Sigma }_N = F(U_{\Gamma }(\eta _{U_{\Gamma } UN})) \circ F(\Delta ^{\Gamma }_{UN})\).

Proof ▶

Coassociativity follows from naturality of \(\Delta ^{\Gamma }\), coassociativity of \(U_{\Gamma }\), and naturality of \(\eta \). Counitality follows from the counit laws of \(U_{\Gamma }\) and both triangle identities of \(F \dashv U\).

Theorem 3.6 Lifted Counit Isomorphism \(\alpha _*\alpha ^* N \cong N\)
✓
#

Uses

  • Theorem 3.5
  • Proposition 3.1

Lean declarations

  • ThesisLean.Section3_2.unstablePushforwardCotensorIso
  • ThesisLean.Section3_2.isIso_unstableLiftedCounit

Assume \(\mathcal{C}^{U_{\Gamma }}\) has equalizers, \(F\) preserves the defining equalizer of \(\alpha ^* N\), and \(\varepsilon : FU \xrightarrow {\sim } 1_{\mathcal{D}}\) is an isomorphism. Then the lifted counit \(\tilde{\varepsilon }_N : \alpha _*(\alpha ^* N) \to N\) is an isomorphism of \(U_{\Sigma }\)-coalgebras.

Proof ▶

On cofree objects, \(\alpha _*(\operatorname {cofree}_{U_{\Gamma }}(UY)) = \operatorname {cofree}_{U_{\Sigma }}(Y)\) holds definitionally. Since \(F\) preserves the equalizer and \(\varepsilon \) is an isomorphism, \(\alpha _*(\alpha ^* N)\) is the equalizer of \((\operatorname {cofree}_{U_{\Sigma }}(\psi _N), \Delta ^{\Sigma }_N)\), which is isomorphic to \(N\) by Proposition 3.1 via the lifted counit \(\tilde{\varepsilon }_N\).

Proposition 3.7 Excess Bounds and Colimit Decomposition

The unstable excess condition \(2p^n {\lt} k + (2p^n - 2)\) holds iff \(k \ge 3\), and the \(b(n)_*\)-action excess condition \(2p^n {\lt} k\) holds iff \(k \ge 2p^n + 1\). In this range, left and right \(v_n^{-1}\)-actions commute with conjugation, and colimit preservation yields \(U_{\Gamma }(K(n)_* \otimes M) \cong B(n)_* \otimes _{K(n)_*} U_{\Sigma }(K(n)_* \otimes M)\).

Proof ▶

Arithmetic bounds verified by linear arithmetic; inversion and colimit isomorphisms verified categorically.

Proposition 3.8 Unstable Cotensor Identification and Height-\(n\) Unit Isomorphism

When \(B(n)_* \otimes _{K(n)_*} -\) preserves the canonical equalizer of \(U_{\Sigma }\)-coalgebras:

  1. \(\alpha ^*(U_{\Sigma }(Y)) \cong U_{\Gamma }(U(Y))\) on cofree coalgebras.

  2. For \(k\)-connected \(M\) with \(k \ge 2p^n + 1\), the underlying object of \(\alpha ^*\alpha _* M\) is isomorphic to \(B(n)_* \otimes _{BP_*} M\).

  3. If \(M\) is also of height \(n\), the lifted unit \(\tilde{\sigma }_M : M \to \alpha ^*\alpha _* M\) is an isomorphism of \(U_{\Gamma }\)-coalgebras.

Proof ▶

Proved by comparing the defining equalizer of \(\alpha ^*\) with the image of the canonical equalizer of Proposition 3.1 under \(B(n)_* \otimes _{K(n)_*} -\).

Theorem 3.9 Unstable Change of Rings
✓
#

Uses

  • Proposition 2.15
  • Proposition 3.8

Lean declarations

  • ThesisLean.Section3_2.unstableChangeOfRingsCobarIso
  • ThesisLean.Section3_2.unstablePullbackCobarLeftContractibleTower

If \(M\) is a \(U_{\Gamma }\)-comodule of height \(n\) and connectivity \(k \ge 2p^n + 1\), then for any test coalgebra \(P \in \mathcal{C}^{U_{\Gamma }}\), there is an isomorphism of coaugmented cosimplicial cobar hom-towers

\[ \operatorname {Hom}_{\mathcal{C}^{U_{\Gamma }}}(P, C^{\bullet }_{U_{\Gamma }}(M)) \; \cong \; \operatorname {Hom}_{\mathcal{D}^{U_{\Sigma }}}(\alpha _* P, C^{\bullet }_{U_{\Sigma }}(\alpha _* M)), \]

and \(\alpha ^*(C^{\bullet }_{U_{\Sigma }}(\alpha _* M))\) has a left-contractible hom-tower into every cofree \(U_{\Gamma }\)-coalgebra. Consequently, \(\operatorname {Ext}_{U_{\Gamma }}(BP_*, M) \cong \operatorname {Ext}_{U_{\Sigma }}(K(n)_*, K(n)_* \otimes _{BP_*} M)\).

Proof ▶

Combines the cobar isomorphism induced by the lifted unit isomorphism \(\tilde{\sigma }_M : M \xrightarrow {\sim } \alpha ^*\alpha _* M\) of Proposition 3.8 with the Generalized Shapiro’s Lemma (Proposition 2.15).
