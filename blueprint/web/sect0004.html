2.2 Morphisms of Comonads and Induced Functors

Definition 2.4 Functor of Comonads
✓
#

Lean declarations

  • ThesisLean.Section2_2.ComonadFunctor

A functor of comonads \((F, \alpha ) : (\mathcal{C}, G) \to (\mathcal{D}, H)\) consists of a functor \(F : \mathcal{C} \to \mathcal{D}\) and a natural transformation \(\alpha : F \circ G \to H \circ F\) satisfying \(\Delta ^H_{FX} \circ \alpha _X = H(\alpha _X) \circ \alpha _{GX} \circ F(\Delta ^G_X)\) and \(\epsilon ^H_{FX} \circ \alpha _X = F(\epsilon ^G_X)\).

Proposition 2.5 Coalgebra Pushforward
✓
#

Uses

  • Definition 2.4

Lean declarations

  • ThesisLean.Section2_2.ComonadFunctor.pushforward

A functor of comonads \((F, \alpha ) : (\mathcal{C}, G) \to (\mathcal{D}, H)\) induces a pushforward functor \(\alpha _* : \mathcal{C}^G \to \mathcal{D}^H\) given on objects by \(\alpha _*(X, \psi _X) = (FX, \alpha _X \circ F(\psi _X))\), commuting strictly with the forgetful functors.

Proof ▶

Coassociativity and counitality of \(\alpha _X \circ F(\psi _X)\) follow directly from the coalgebra axioms for \((X, \psi _X)\), naturality of \(\alpha \), and Definition 2.4.

Definition 2.6 Opfunctor of Comonads
✓
#

Lean declarations

  • ThesisLean.Section2_2.ComonadOpfunctor

An opfunctor of comonads \((U, \beta ) : (\mathcal{D}, H) \to (\mathcal{C}, G)\) consists of a functor \(U : \mathcal{D} \to \mathcal{C}\) and a natural transformation \(\beta : G \circ U \to U \circ H\) satisfying \(U(\Delta ^H_Y) \circ \beta _Y = \beta _{HY} \circ G(\beta _Y) \circ \Delta ^G_{UY}\) and \(U(\epsilon ^H_Y) \circ \beta _Y = \epsilon ^G_{UY}\).

Proposition 2.7 Cokleisli Pushforward
✓
#

Uses

  • Definition 2.6

Lean declarations

  • ThesisLean.Section2_2.ComonadOpfunctor.cokleisliPushforward

An opfunctor of comonads \((U, \beta ) : (\mathcal{D}, H) \to (\mathcal{C}, G)\) induces a functor \(\beta _* : \mathcal{D}_H \to \mathcal{C}_G\) given by \(\beta _*(Y) = UY\) and \(\beta _*(f : HY \to Y') = U(f) \circ \beta _Y\).

Proof ▶

Preservation of identities and composition follows from the two opfunctor axioms of Definition 2.6.

Proposition 2.8 Coalgebra Pullback via Equalizers
✓
#

Uses

  • Definition 2.6

Lean declarations

  • ThesisLean.Section2_2.ComonadOpfunctor.coalgebraPullback

Assume \(\mathcal{C}^G\) has equalizers. Any opfunctor of comonads \((U, \beta ) : (\mathcal{D}, H) \to (\mathcal{C}, G)\) induces a pullback functor \(\alpha ^* : \mathcal{D}^H \to \mathcal{C}^G\) defined on \((Y, \psi _Y)\) as the equalizer in \(\mathcal{C}^G\) of \(\operatorname {cofree}_G(U\psi _Y)\) and \(G(\beta _Y) \circ \Delta ^G_{UY} : \operatorname {cofree}_G(UY) \rightrightarrows \operatorname {cofree}_G(UHY)\).

Proof ▶

Constructed via the limit equalizer of the parallel pair of cofree \(G\)-coalgebra morphisms.
