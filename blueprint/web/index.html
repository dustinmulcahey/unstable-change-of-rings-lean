An Unstable Variant of the Morava Change of Rings Theorem for \(K(n)\) Theory

Dustin Mulcahey

This site is the interactive Lean 4 Blueprint for Dustin Mulcahey's Ph.D. thesis, “An unstable variant of the Morava Change of Rings theorem for \(K(n)\) theory” (The Graduate Center, CUNY; Advisors: Robert Thompson & Martin Bendersky). Every categorical definition, proposition, and theorem in Chapters 2 and 3 has been completely formalized and verified with zero sorry axioms in Lean 4 + Mathlib4.
