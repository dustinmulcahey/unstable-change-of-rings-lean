Given \(F \dashv U\) with unit \(\sigma : 1_{\mathcal{C}} \to UF\) and counit \(\tau : FU \to 1_{\mathcal{D}}\), and a comonad functor \((F, \alpha ) : (\mathcal{C}, G) \to (\mathcal{D}, H)\), the mate transformation \(\beta _Y := U(H(\tau _Y) \circ \alpha _{UY}) \circ \sigma _{GUY}\) satisfies

\[ \tau _{HY} \circ F(\beta _Y) = H(\tau _Y) \circ \alpha _{UY} \quad \text{and}\quad \beta _{FX} \circ G(\sigma _X) = U(\alpha _X) \circ \sigma _{GX}. \]
Proof ▶

Proved using naturality of \(\tau , \sigma , \alpha \) and the triangle identities for \(F \dashv U\).

Proposition 2.11 Lifted Adjunction on Coalgebras
✓
#

  • Proposition 2.8
  • Proposition 2.10

Lean declarations

  • ThesisLean.Section2_3.liftedAdjunction

When \(\mathcal{C}^G\) has equalizers, the pushforward \(\alpha _* : \mathcal{C}^G \to \mathcal{D}^H\) is left adjoint to the pullback \(\alpha ^* : \mathcal{D}^H \to \mathcal{C}^G\).

Proof ▶

Constructed by lifting the unit \(\sigma _X\) into the equalizer \(\alpha ^*\alpha _* X\) via \(G(\sigma _X) \circ \psi _X\), lifting the counit \(\tau _Y\) via \(\tau _Y \circ F(\epsilon ^G_{UY} \circ \iota _Y)\), and verifying both triangle identities in \(\mathcal{D}^H\) and \(\mathcal{C}^G\).
