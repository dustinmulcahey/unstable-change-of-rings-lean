Definition 2.4Functor of Comonads

A functor of comonads \((F, \alpha ) : (\mathcal{C}, G) \to (\mathcal{D}, H)\) consists of a functor \(F : \mathcal{C} \to \mathcal{D}\) and a natural transformation \(\alpha : F \circ G \to H \circ F\) satisfying \(\Delta ^H_{FX} \circ \alpha _X = H(\alpha _X) \circ \alpha _{GX} \circ F(\Delta ^G_X)\) and \(\epsilon ^H_{FX} \circ \alpha _X = F(\epsilon ^G_X)\).

Definition 2.6Opfunctor of Comonads

An opfunctor of comonads \((U, \beta ) : (\mathcal{D}, H) \to (\mathcal{C}, G)\) consists of a functor \(U : \mathcal{D} \to \mathcal{C}\) and a natural transformation \(\beta : G \circ U \to U \circ H\) satisfying \(U(\Delta ^H_Y) \circ \beta _Y = \beta _{HY} \circ G(\beta _Y) \circ \Delta ^G_{UY}\) and \(U(\epsilon ^H_Y) \circ \beta _Y = \epsilon ^G_{UY}\).

Proposition 3.7Excess Bounds and Colimit Decomposition

The unstable excess condition \(2p^n {\lt} k + (2p^n - 2)\) holds iff \(k \ge 3\), and the \(b(n)_*\)-action excess condition \(2p^n {\lt} k\) holds iff \(k \ge 2p^n + 1\). In this range, left and right \(v_n^{-1}\)-actions commute with conjugation, and colimit preservation yields \(U_{\Gamma }(K(n)_* \otimes M) \cong B(n)_* \otimes _{K(n)_*} U_{\Sigma }(K(n)_* \otimes M)\).

Proposition 2.15Generalized Shapiro’s Lemma for Comonads

Assume there is a functor \(W : \mathcal{D} \to \mathcal{C}\) and a natural isomorphism \(U_G \circ \alpha ^* \cong W \circ U_H\). Then:

  1. For every \(N \in \mathcal{D}^H\) and \(J \in \mathcal{C}\), the hom-tower \(\operatorname {Hom}_{\mathcal{C}^G}(\alpha ^*(C^{\bullet }_H(N)), G(J)) \to \operatorname {Hom}_{\mathcal{C}^G}(\alpha ^* N, G(J))\) is left-contractible, so \(\alpha ^* N \to \alpha ^*(C^{\bullet }_H(N))\) is a Bousfield weak \(G\)-resolution.

  2. For every \(M \in \mathcal{C}^G\) and \(N \in \mathcal{D}^H\), there is an isomorphism of coaugmented cosimplicial hom-towers \(\operatorname {Hom}_{\mathcal{C}^G}(M, \alpha ^*(C^{\bullet }_H(N))) \cong \operatorname {Hom}_{\mathcal{D}^H}(\alpha _* M, C^{\bullet }_H(N))\), inducing \(\operatorname {Ext}_G(M, \alpha ^* N) \cong \operatorname {Ext}_H(\alpha _* M, N)\).
